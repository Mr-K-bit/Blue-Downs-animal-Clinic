Draft Privacy Policy

Information collected and why

If enabled after approval, enquiry forms may request an owner’s name, contact details, pet’s name and type, reason for contact, preferred appointment details, and optional notes. The clinic should collect only information needed to respond, manage enquiries, or provide services, and explain the purpose before collection.

Processing, storage, and providers

Information may be processed by the clinic and contracted website, hosting, email, or other service providers. Before launch, the clinic must identify providers, put suitable safeguards and contractual terms in place, restrict access, use HTTPS, and set a documented retention and secure deletion process. This prototype does not send or store form information.

Rights and contact

People may have rights under applicable law to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, and to object to certain processing. The clinic should confirm its process and response contact. Information Officer: to be confirmed by clinic management.

This draft does not claim POPIA compliance and is not legal advice. Confirm lawful grounds, notice and consent mechanisms, retention, cross-border processing, security safeguards, and breach procedures with the clinic and a South African legal professional.

Draft Website Terms & Conditions

This website is intended to provide general information and contact details. Its content must be kept accurate and approved by the clinic. It is not a substitute for professional veterinary advice, diagnosis, or emergency care.

Enquiry submission does not create an appointment, client relationship, or guarantee of availability. If online transactions are introduced, the clinic should review ECTA disclosures and consumer terms, including the business’s legal identity and contact details, service characteristics, prices and charges, payment, cancellations, refunds, disputes, privacy, and payment security.

Final terms, intellectual-property permissions, and any liability language require clinic and South African legal review.

Draft Appointment & Cancellation Policy

Submitting an appointment enquiry does not confirm or reserve a booking. Before live use, the clinic must explain its confirmation method, response expectations, rescheduling and cancellation process, any applicable charges, and missed-appointment terms.

Emergency availability and after-hours arrangements must be verified. For urgent situations, visitors should contact an appropriate veterinary provider and not wait for an online response.

Draft Website Disclaimer

Website content is for general information only and may be incomplete or awaiting confirmation. It is not a veterinary diagnosis or treatment recommendation. For concerns about an animal’s health, contact an appropriately qualified veterinary professional. For urgent situations, seek veterinary help immediately.

Do not rely on draft hours, services, credentials, or contact details until the clinic verifies them. This disclaimer requires review and does not replace legal advice.

PAIA checklist & notice

Confirm the clinic’s status and obligations before publishing a notice or manual. This proposal does not claim an existing PAIA manual, registration, or compliance status.
